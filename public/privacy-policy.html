Privacy Policy

Khanyisa Transcribers is committed to protecting personal information responsibly and lawfully. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose and protect information provided by clients, authorised parties and website visitors.

1. Information we collect

We may collect personal information including names, contact details, identity information, matter-related information, audio files, legal instructions, and any other information relevant to the services requested.

2. How we use information

Personal information is used to provide transcription, translation, recording and administrative services, communicate with clients, manage job requests, maintain quality control and ensure compliance with legal and professional obligations.

3. Lawful basis and consent

We process personal information only where required for the provision of services, to carry out valid instructions, to satisfy legal obligations, or where consent has been lawfully obtained for specific processing activities.

4. Storage and security

We use reasonable administrative, technical and organisational measures to protect personal information from loss, misuse, unauthorised access and disclosure. Access is limited to authorised personnel for legitimate business purposes.

5. Disclosure

We may disclose personal information where required by law, court process, legal instruction, or with client consent. We do not sell personal information and only share information with trusted third parties where necessary to deliver the service.

6. Access and correction

Clients may request access to their personal information, ask for corrections, or raise concerns regarding processing. We will respond in line with applicable legal requirements and internal procedures.

7. Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected, to comply with legal obligations, and to support legitimate business needs.

8. Contact

If you have questions or requests relating to this Privacy Policy, please contact the business directly using the details provided on the website contact section.
