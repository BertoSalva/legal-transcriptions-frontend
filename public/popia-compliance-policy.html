POPIA Compliance Policy

Khanyisa Transcribers recognises its responsibility to process personal information in a lawful, secure and transparent manner in terms of the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and related obligations.

1. Purpose and scope

This policy applies to all personal information collected, received, stored, processed, used or disclosed by Khanyisa Transcribers in relation to clients, contractors, website visitors and any authorised parties connected to legal and administrative matters.

2. Lawful processing

We process personal information only under a lawful basis. This may include compliance with contractual obligations, legal obligations, legitimate interests, or consent where required and appropriate.

3. Collection limitation

We collect only the personal information needed for the services requested and limit access to that information to staff who require it to perform their duties responsibly and professionally.

4. Purpose specification

Information is collected for clearly defined business purposes, including transcription, translation, legal support, communication, file management, billing, compliance and record-keeping.

5. Data quality and retention

We take reasonable steps to keep personal information accurate, complete and up to date and to retain it only for the period necessary to fulfil the stated purpose or meet legal obligations.

6. Security safeguards

We maintain appropriate technical and organisational controls to protect personal information from unauthorised access, loss, damage, alteration or destruction. This includes confidentiality protocols, limited access, secure storage practices and operational controls.

7. Rights of data subjects

Individuals may request access to their personal information, request correction of inaccurate data, object to certain processing, or ask for the deletion or restriction of certain information, subject to legal exceptions and internal approval procedures.

8. Third-party processing

Where personal information is shared with trusted service providers or authorised parties, we ensure that such processing is limited to the stated purpose and supported by appropriate safeguards and contractual controls.

9. Breach response

We have procedures in place to detect, assess, respond to and mitigate any data security incident or breach in line with applicable legal duties and internal reporting obligations.

10. Contact

If you have questions, complaints or requests regarding this POPIA Compliance Policy or the handling of your personal information, please contact Khanyisa Transcribers through the contact channels provided on the website.
